Traditional Mandi Supply Chain vs. FarmDirect

See how removing layers of unnecessary middlemen transforms both farmer income and customer freshness.

❌ Traditional Middleman Chain

Farmer Gets only 15% - 20% of retail price
Local Agent Deducts 8% commission + loading fee
Wholesaler Sits in hot mandi yards for 3 - 5 days
Distributor Gassed with ethylene or artificial wax
Retailer Sells stale produce at 400% inflated price

Result: Farmer in debt, produce loses 60% of vital nutrients.

✅ FarmDirect Model

Farmer Receives 85% - 95% of full consumer price
FarmDirect Hub Direct solar-powered temperature check
Customer Enjoys 24-hr sunrise crisp produce at fair rates

Result: Thriving rural farmers, peak kitchen taste and zero food waste.
