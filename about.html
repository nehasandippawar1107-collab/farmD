🌾 Our Origin & Purpose

Bridging the Gap Between
Soil and Soul.

In traditional retail, the farmer who toils under the scorching sun for four months receives merely ₹10 to ₹15 for a kilo of produce that sells for ₹60 in metropolitan supermarkets. FarmDirect was born to end this injustice once and for all.

Taste the Direct Harvest Partner as a Grower
🎯

Our Mission

To build India's most transparent, community-owned agricultural network that guarantees dignified earnings to family farmers while delivering nutrient-dense, chemical-free food directly to customer doorsteps within 24 hours of harvest.

🌍

Our Vision

A regenerative food system where smallholder growers retain 100% pricing autonomy, urban households know the face and village behind every meal, and post-harvest produce waste is reduced to near zero through precision order-to-harvest logistics.

Systemic Problem Solved

The Ruthless Middleman Markup

Understanding where your grocery money really went before FarmDirect came along.

Layer 1: Village Aggregators

Exploits lack of local cold storage by forcing farmers to perform distressed sales at arbitrary low rates.

Layer 2: Mandi Commission Agents

Charges auction cuts, weighing cuts, and delayed payment fees. Growers wait 60+ days for their own sales cash.

Layer 3: City Transit Dealers

Loads produce onto hot trucks where 35% of freshness is destroyed. Applies artificial wax and ripening chemicals.

The FarmDirect Antidote

Direct mobile cataloging. Customer places order → Farmer harvests next sunrise → FarmDirect delivers directly.
