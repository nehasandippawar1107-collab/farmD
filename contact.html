Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

How do you guarantee farmers get paid fairly?

Unlike traditional mandis where middlemen deduct 30% to 50% commission, FarmDirect charges 0% commission to smallholder farmers. When a customer pays, 100% of the produce price goes directly to the grower's designated bank account via automated UPI settlements within 24 hours of successful delivery.

What happens if my produce arrives damaged or bruised?

We offer our "FarmDirect 100% Crisp Guarantee". If any fruits or vegetables do not meet high harvest standards, simply take a quick photo and contact us via the Order Tracking page or phone helpline for an instant replacement or 100% immediate refund.

How do farmers list their crops on the platform?

Farmers can sign up via our Farmer Registration portal. Our local village coordinators verify farm soil tests and land tenure. Once verified, farmers can add crops, specify harvest dates, and adjust quantities in real time through their simplified mobile dashboard.

What are your delivery slots and cold-chain protocols?

We deliver in three convenient time slots: Sunrise (6:00 AM – 9:00 AM), Mid-Day (12:00 PM – 3:00 PM), and Evening (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM). All produce travels in recyclable temperature-controlled cartons that maintain a steady 4°C to 8°C chill throughout transit.
